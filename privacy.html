Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Lapster: The before and after Master ("we", "us", or "our") operates as a Progressive Web App (PWA). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect personal information. The app runs entirely in your browser. Trial status and license information are stored locally in your device's localStorage and are never sent to any server.

Local Storage

We use localStorage only to remember:

  • Whether you have started the 2-day free trial and the start date
  • Whether you have purchased lifetime access

This data stays on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

Cookies

We do not use cookies.

Third-Party Services

This is a client-side only application. No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app support channels.
