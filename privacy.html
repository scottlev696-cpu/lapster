Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Lapster: The before and after Master (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information when you use the web app and the packaged Android app distributed via Google Play.

Information We Collect

We do not require accounts. The app stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Trial start date and license status (stored in localStorage / app preferences)
  • Any before/after images you create (stored only on your device)

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data, photos, or usage analytics to any server.

Payments

The 2-day free trial and the one-time $4.99 Lifetime Access purchase are processed by Google Play Billing when you install the app from the Play Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. See Google’s privacy policy for billing information.

Permissions

When packaged as an Android app the only permissions requested are those needed for the core function (photo access if you choose to import images). No location, contacts, or background tracking.

Third Parties

The hosted web version is served via GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs. The packaged app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions related to Lapster: The before and after Master, please use the support contact listed on the Google Play listing.
